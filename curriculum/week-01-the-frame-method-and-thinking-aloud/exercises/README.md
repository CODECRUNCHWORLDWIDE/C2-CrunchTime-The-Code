# Week 1 — Exercises (FRAME Drills)

Five problems. For each, you will:

1. Read the prompt.
2. Solve the problem yourself **with FRAME** in <30 minutes per problem, ideally with a recorder running.
3. Write up your solution using [`frame_template.md`](frame_template.md).
4. Run the [`timed_runner.py`](timed_runner.py) against your code to verify it passes.

**Total time estimate:** ~6 hours for all five (1–1.5 hr each).

## The five drills (in order)

| # | Problem | Sub-shape | Difficulty |
|---|---------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | [Reverse the Siding](exercise-01-reverse-the-siding.md) | Converging (swap) | Easy |
| 2 | [The Mirror Serial](exercise-02-mirror-serial.md) | Converging (skip and compare) | Easy/Med |
| 3 | [The Widest Ballast Pair](exercise-03-widest-ballast-pair.md) | Converging (sorted pair search) | Easy/Med |
| 4 | [The Stuck Gauge](exercise-04-stuck-gauge.md) | Same-direction (read / write) | Easy/Med |
| 5 | [The Market Awning](exercise-05-market-awning.md) | Converging (tracked max, greedy move) | Medium |

## How to work each drill

- **Open a timer.** Set it for 30 minutes.
- **Open a recorder.** Phone voice memo is fine. Don't worry about audio quality.
- **Open the drill file** in this folder.
- **Read the prompt.** Do not scroll past it.
- **Start FRAME.** Out loud. The whole way — all five steps, in order.
- **Save your solution** as `drill-NN-solution.py` in your portfolio repo, in a `c2-week-01/` subfolder.
- **Write up using the template.** Save as `drill-NN.md` in your portfolio repo.
- **Run the tests.** `pytest c2-week-01/`.

When all five drills are written up, your portfolio looks like:

That's the start of your interview-prep portfolio.

## A note on listening back

The first time you record yourself doing FRAME, **you will hate the recording.** That's universal. You'll hear "uh" a lot, you'll trail off, you'll mumble. Don't delete it. **Listen back at 1.5×** and write three notes about what to improve next time. That's the entire feedback loop.

By exercise 5, you will sound better than exercise 1. By the end of Week 4 (after your first mock), you will sound like a candidate.
