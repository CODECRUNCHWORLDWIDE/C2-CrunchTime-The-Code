# Week 2 — Challenges

Two stretch problems. Optional, but the gap between "comfortable hash-map user" and "interview-ready" lives here.

1. **[Challenge 1 — The Balanced Shift](challenge-01-balanced-shifts.md)** — prefix sums plus a hash map carrying two payloads; the canonical mid-level discriminator. ~90 minutes.
2. **[Challenge 2 — The Texture Residency Board](challenge-02-residency-board.md)** — `OrderedDict`, or a hash map composed with a doubly-linked list; the canonical design problem. ~120 minutes.

Do them in order. The second is markedly harder than the first and crosses from algorithms into design, where the thing being graded is whether your invariants survive every method rather than whether your loop terminates.
