# Week 4 — Challenges

One hard challenge this week. It combines **three** sub-patterns into a single problem — the kind of multi-step composition that appears in real onsite interviews.

| # | Challenge | Sub-patterns | Difficulty | Target solve time |
|---|-----------|--------------|------------|------------------:|
| 1 | [Reorder Linked List](./challenge-01-reorder-linked-list.md) | Fast/slow midpoint + reverse list + merge two lists | Medium-Hard | 60 min |

Do Drills 1–4 first. The challenge reuses **Drill 3 (find middle)** as its first sub-step. The other two sub-steps (reverse, merge) are likely already in your toolkit from prior practice; this challenge stitches them together.
