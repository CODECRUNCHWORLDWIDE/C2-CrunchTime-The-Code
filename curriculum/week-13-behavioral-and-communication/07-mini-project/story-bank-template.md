# Story Bank — Template

> Copy this file to `behavioral/c2-week-13/story-bank/story-bank.md` in your portfolio and fill in your own true stories. This is a **scaffold, not content** — every story must be real and yours. Keep entries tight: these are skeletons you deliver *from*, not essays you read aloud. Fill all twelve slots; slots 1–11 are full STAR stories, slot 12 is the opener.

Reminder of the discipline (see Lecture 1):
- **Situation** ≈ one to two sentences. **Task** = your piece, in "I." **Action** = the bulk, first-person, concrete. **Result** = a metric or a concrete observable, plus the lesson — never an adjective.
- **Tags** = every category this story answers, each with a one-line note on how you frame it for that category.
- **Time the spoken version.** Record whether it lands under 2:00.

## Bank-readiness checklist

- [ ] All twelve slots filled with **real, true** stories.
- [ ] Every Result is a metric or a concrete observable — zero adjectives ("it went well") used as a Result.
- [ ] Every Action is first-person ("I"), with the team credited but not erasing your contribution.
- [ ] Every story timed aloud; any over 2:00 is flagged and trimmed.
- [ ] Each tag carries a one-line framing note (not just a category name).
- [ ] You can name the handle for any category in under three seconds (drill with the index-card test from Lecture 2 §2).

When every box is checked, build the [coverage matrix](./coverage-matrix-template.md) and verify the two acceptance rules.
