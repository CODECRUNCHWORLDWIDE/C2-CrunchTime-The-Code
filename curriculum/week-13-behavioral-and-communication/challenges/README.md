# Week 13 — Challenges

Two behavioral challenges. Like the exercises, these are **not coding problems** — they are live-skill drills that the written exercises cannot fully build. Challenge 1 rehearses recovery from a wrong story mid-answer; Challenge 2 produces a polished, specific follow-up email. Both are graded against rubrics in their respective files.

| # | File | Skill | Deliverable | Target time |
|---|------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | [challenge-01-recovery-drill.md](./challenge-01-recovery-drill.md) | Recovering from a mis-mapped story | A transcript/reflection of three rehearsed recoveries | 60 min |
| 2 | [challenge-02-follow-up-email.md](./challenge-02-follow-up-email.md) | The post-interview follow-up email | A polished email + a short rationale | 45 min |

**How to use:**

1. **Challenge 1 is best done with a peer**, but a recorder works solo. You will deliberately start the *wrong* story for a prompt and practice each of the three recovery moves from Lecture 3 §1. The deliverable is a short reflection on which move felt most natural and where you still panicked.
2. **Challenge 2** is a written deliverable: a real or realistic follow-up email, written against the anatomy in Lecture 3 §5, plus two or three sentences explaining your choices.

Do Challenge 1 first — recovery is the harder skill and benefits from a fresh, un-tired session. Challenge 2 is calmer work and pairs well with the end of a study block.

Both deliverables belong in your portfolio under `behavioral/c2-week-13/challenges/`. The recovery reflection and the email are artifacts a future-you will reuse before every real loop.
