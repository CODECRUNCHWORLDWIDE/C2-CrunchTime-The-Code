| 2 | [challenge-02-hostile-and-curveball-round.md](./challenge-02-hostile-and-curveball-round.md) | The hard questions — weakness, why-leaving, failure-with-a-twist, illegal/inappropriate, and curveballs — with worked responses and the recovery framework |

Challenge 1 is the **required shipping deliverable** for the week — a full mock behavioral round, recorded, with two-pass self-feedback notes committed to your portfolio. It is the behavioral equivalent of the recorded mock you ran in Phase 1, and it is the dress rehearsal for the behavioral round inside Mock #3 next week.

Challenge 2 is a focused drill on the questions that throw people. It is shorter than Challenge 1 and uses the same record-and-review protocol. Do Challenge 1 on Friday and Challenge 2 on Saturday, so your story bank is assembled before you stress-test the hard questions against it.

Both challenges use the **two-pass self-feedback protocol** from the Phase-1 mock: watch your recording once for *content* (did each answer surface signal, land a number, hold the "I") and once for *delivery* (pace, filler words, eye contact if on video, energy). Two passes, two different lenses — the same way you reviewed your coding-solve recordings.
