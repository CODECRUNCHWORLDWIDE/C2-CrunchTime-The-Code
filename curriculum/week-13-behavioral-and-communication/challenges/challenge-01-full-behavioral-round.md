# Challenge 1 — The Full 45-Minute Mock Behavioral Round

> *Simulate a complete behavioral interview round: 6–8 questions across the categories, delivered cold, recorded end to end, then reviewed in two passes against the rubric. This is the required shipping deliverable for Week 13 and the dress rehearsal for the behavioral round inside Mock #3 next week.*

**The deliverable.** A recording of a full 45-minute mock behavioral round, plus your two-pass self-feedback notes, committed to `behavioral/mock-rounds/round-01.md` (notes) with the recording linked. By the end you should be able to deliver any of these answers from your story bank without notes.

---

## Setup

You have two ways to run this, in order of preference:

1. **With a partner (best).** Pair with another C2 learner. They play the interviewer, reading the questions below in order, taking brief notes, and throwing one or two natural follow-ups per answer ("what specifically was your role?", "what would you do differently?"). Then swap and you interview them. Interviewing someone else is the fastest way to internalize the rubric — you will *hear* the missing "I" and the buried result in their answers, then catch them in yours.

2. **Solo (works fine).** Record yourself. Read each question aloud (or have a text-to-speech read it), pause, then answer from memory as if a real interviewer asked it. Hit record once and run the whole 45 minutes in one take — the point is to simulate the *endurance* of a real round, not to splice together perfect individual answers.

Either way: **one continuous recording, ~45 minutes, no stopping to re-do an answer.** Real rounds do not let you re-take. The re-take happens in review, not in the room.

---

## The questions (read in this order)

A real behavioral round is 6–8 questions across categories, usually opening with "tell me about yourself," moving through the meaty categories, and closing with "why us" and "your questions for me." This set mirrors that arc. Budget ~5–6 minutes per question including follow-ups.

1. **(Why-this-role / opener)** "Let's start with you — walk me through your background and what brings you here." *(Target: your 90-second "tell me about yourself" pitch. Practice landing it under 90 seconds.)*
2. **(Accomplishment)** "Tell me about the work you're proudest of." *(Deploy your strongest-number story — debugging or checkout-latency.)*
3. **(Conflict)** "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker or your manager." *(Deploy your conflict story. Land the relationship outcome.)*
4. **(Failure)** "Tell me about a time you failed." *(Deploy your failure story. Own it; name the change. Be ready for the "what would you do with more time?" follow-up.)*
5. **(Leadership / influence)** "Tell me about a time you drove something forward without the authority to make people do it." *(Deploy your leadership story. The proof is that it stuck without you.)*
6. **(Ambiguity)** "Tell me about a time you had to make progress with unclear requirements." *(Deploy your ambiguity story. Make the explicit assumption visible.)*
7. **(Difficult person / Teamwork)** "Tell me about a difficult person you worked with, or a time teamwork was hard." *(Deploy a difficult-person or teamwork story. Empathy, not blame.)*
8. **(Why-us / close)** "Why this team, and what questions do you have for me?" *(Have two specific, genuine questions ready. 'Do you have questions?' is graded too — having none reads as disengaged.)*

If you run short on stories for a category, that is itself the finding — note the gap and fill it in the mini-project. You should *not* tell the same story for two different questions; if you find yourself reaching for the checkout-latency story for both #2 and #6, you have found a coverage hole.

## The rubric

Score each answer 0–2 on each of the five signals (0 = absent, 1 = present but weak, 2 = clearly surfaced). Max 10 per answer.

| Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|--------|---|---|---|
| Ownership | All "we"; you watched it happen | Some "I", but role is fuzzy | Clear "I"; you drove it, decided, risked |
| Impact | No outcome stated | Vague outcome ("it went well") | A specific number tied to something that mattered |
| Self-awareness | None | A token "I'd do better" | A genuine trade-off or limit you'd revisit |
| Collaboration | Solo heroics or no people | People mentioned, role unclear | "We" for the outcome; credit shared; friction resolved |
| Growth | No lesson | Generic lesson ("be more careful") | A concrete habit/process you adopted, ideally proven |

Also track, per answer:

- **Length** — was it under ~100 seconds? Over two minutes is a rambling flag.
- **S/T over-spend** — did you start the Action within the first 20 seconds?
- **Structure** — did you signal the STAR shape, or free-associate?

A round averaging **8+/10 across answers** is interview-ready. **6–7** means a few categories need a sharper story or a tighter budget. **Below 6** means re-drill the weak categories before Mock #3.

## The two-pass self-feedback protocol

Same protocol as the Phase-1 mock recording. Watch your recording **twice, with two different lenses.**

**Pass 1 — Content (sound off if you want).** For each answer, score the five signals on the rubric above. Specifically catch:

- Where did the "I" disappear into "we"?
- Which answers ended without a number?
- Which answers buried the result under a long Situation?
- Did you tell any story twice (a coverage gap)?

**Pass 2 — Delivery (watch/listen at 1×).** Ignore content; watch *how* you came across:

- Pace — too fast (nervous) or too slow (rambling)?
- Filler words — count the "ums," "likes," "kind ofs." Everyone has some; note the rate.
- Energy — did you sound engaged or flat? The proudest-work answer especially should have energy.
- If on video: eye contact, posture, whether you looked like you were reading.

Write up both passes in `behavioral/mock-rounds/round-01.md`: the per-answer rubric scores, your three biggest content fixes, and your three biggest delivery fixes. Then **re-record your two weakest answers** with the fixes applied.

---

## Acceptance criteria

- One continuous ~45-minute recording, linked from `behavioral/mock-rounds/round-01.md`.
- Per-answer rubric scores (the table above) for all 6–8 questions.
- A two-pass write-up: three content fixes, three delivery fixes.
- Two weakest answers re-recorded with fixes.
- A note on any coverage gap you discovered (a category with no strong story), carried into the mini-project.
