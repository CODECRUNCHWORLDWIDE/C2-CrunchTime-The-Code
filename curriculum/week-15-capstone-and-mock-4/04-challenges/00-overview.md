The challenges build the **dynamic** ones — the outreach that starts conversations and the mock that proves the skill holds under pressure. Both produce something you act on immediately: the outreach is sent (first message by Sunday), and the mock is the course's exit measurement.

Allocate the full target time. Do not rush the target list — an untargeted list poisons every downstream step. Do not skip the Mock #4 recording — it is non-negotiable for the capstone.
