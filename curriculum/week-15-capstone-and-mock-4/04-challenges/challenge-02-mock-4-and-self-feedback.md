# Challenge 2 — Mock #4 and the Self-Feedback Note

**Skill:** the exit mock (Lecture 2).
**Produces:** a recorded Mock #4 plus the self-feedback note that measures whether prior behavior changes stuck.
**Target time:** 120 minutes (45 mock + 75 watching and writing).
**Required.**

## The task

Run the course's final mock under full real-interview conditions — a peer or platform partner (no solo flavor), a 90-second behavioral open, an uncurated UMPIRE problem under a hard 45-minute clock, a behavioral close, all recorded — then watch it back with the two-pass protocol and write the self-feedback note whose headline section is the verdict on whether your three prior behavior changes held.

This is the exit measurement of the entire course. Every UMPIRE rep, every prior mock, every behavior change funnels into this one 45-minute slot. Run it honestly.

## Setup (from Lecture 2 §2)

- **Partner confirmed** — peer or Pramp / interviewing.io. No solo flavor for Mock #4.
- **Recording rig tested** — screen + face + audio; 30-second test played back.
- **The three prior behavior changes on a card** where you can see them but the interviewer cannot.
- **90-second self-intro rehearsed once, timed.**
- **One STAR story loaded** for the behavioral close.
- **Coding environment** — CoderPad or shared VS Code, not your personal IDE.

## Run the mock (45 minutes + bookends)

Follow the structure in [Lecture 2 §3](../02-lecture-notes/02-mock-4-the-exit-interview.md#3-the-45-minute-structure-with-bookends):

| Phase | Time |
|-------|------|
| Behavioral open | 90 sec |
| U / M / P | 10 min |
| I (implement) | 18 min |
| R (review) | 10 min |
| E (evaluate) | 5 min |
| Behavioral close | 5 min |

Hard stop at the clock. Record the whole thing including both bookends.

## Watch it back (two-pass, from Week 4 §7)

- **Pass 1** — full recording at 1.5×, dropping 10–15 timestamps for noticeable moments. Flag the **bookends specifically** (the open and the close are the parts you have practiced least).
- **Pass 2** — only the flagged moments at 1.0×, writing observation-then-prescription for each.

## Write the self-feedback note

Use the structure in [Lecture 2 §6](../02-lecture-notes/02-mock-4-the-exit-interview.md#6-the-self-feedback-note--measuring-whether-the-changes-stuck). The **headline section** is the table verdict on your three prior behavior changes:

| Change (from Mock #N) | Held under Mock #4 pressure? | Evidence (timestamp) |
|-----------------------|------------------------------|----------------------|
| ... | Yes / Partial / No | mm:ss — ... |

Then: what the recording shows; the behavioral bookends graded (did the open's thread land? was the close's Result specific?); and the one thing to keep drilling post-course (the change that cracked under exit-mock pressure).

Ship the note to `mocks/mock-04/self-feedback.md` in your portfolio repo, with `recording-link.md`, `immediate-notes.md`, and `timestamps.md` alongside it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Mock #4 run with a partner** (peer or platform), not solo.
- [ ] **Recorded** — screen + face + audio — including both behavioral bookends.
- [ ] **90-second open delivered** (the self-intro with a thread, timed to ~90 sec).
- [ ] **Behavioral close answered in STAR** with a specific, ideally quantified Result.
- [ ] **Two-pass watching** completed; bookends flagged.
- [ ] **Self-feedback note** shipped, with the headline table verdict on the three prior behavior changes.
- [ ] **One "keep drilling" item** named — the change that cracked under pressure, to carry into the real loop.

## How this challenge is graded

| Dimension | Weight | What "yes" looks like |
|-----------|-------:|----------------------|
| Ran the full-fidelity protocol | 25% | Partner, recorded, both bookends, hard clock |
| The technical middle | 20% | UMPIRE run end to end; Match memo under 30 sec; Evaluate reached |
| The behavioral bookends | 20% | Open's thread landed; close's Result was specific |
| The self-feedback verdict | 25% | The three-change table with timestamped evidence; honest verdicts |
| The "keep drilling" item | 10% | One specific, testable item named for the real loop |

The self-feedback verdict carries the most weight because the *measurement* is the point of the exit mock. A mock you ran but did not honestly grade teaches nothing. The verdict — which behavior changes held, which cracked — is the artifact you take into the real interviews.

## Why this challenge

Mock #4 is the last controlled rep before the stakes are real. Every crack it reveals is one you would rather find now than in a real onsite with an offer on the line. Run it hunting for the crack, not for the victory lap. The honesty of this self-feedback note is the difference between walking into the real loop confident-and-prepared versus confident-and-blindsided.
