# Week 15 — Exercises (Capstone Drills)

Four drills. Unlike the algorithm weeks, these are not coding problems — they are the build steps that turn fifteen weeks of work into the capstone portfolio. Each produces a concrete artifact that lands in your repo. Do them in order; each feeds the next, and all four feed the mini-project and the homework.

| # | Drill | Output | Est. time |
|---|-------|--------|----------:|
| 1 | [Portfolio audit](./exercise-01-portfolio-audit.md) | Every write-up audited against the six-point quality bar; failures fixed; the dashboard count made true | 2.5 h |
| 2 | [System-design write-up](./exercise-02-system-design-writeup.md) | `system-design/url-shortener.md` — the junior-level URL-shortener design write-up | 2.5 h |
| 3 | [Recruiter-prep pack](./exercise-03-recruiter-prep-pack.md) | `recruiter-prep/` — resume audit, tiered target-company list, outreach + follow-up templates | 2 h |
| 4 | [Pre-onsite 4-week plan](./exercise-04-pre-onsite-4-week-plan.md) | `study-plan/pre-onsite-4-weeks.md` — your personalized last-mile plan | 1.5 h |

---

## How to work these

- **Exercise 1 first, on Monday.** The audit tells you how many quality write-ups you actually have, which sets the target for the new ones the homework and mocks produce. Do not write new write-ups until you know the audit count — you may find you have 55 passing and only need a handful more.
- **Exercise 2 on Tuesday.** The URL-shortener design write-up is the only "new content" drill of the week — it is the junior-level system-design artifact the capstone requires, built on the Week 12 design intro. Exercise 2 also directly prepares Challenge 2 (the system-design mock).
- **Drills 3 and 4 on Wednesday.** The recruiter pack and the four-week plan are writing tasks, not problem-solving. They are fast if you have the inputs (a draft resume, your weakness diagnosis); budget more if you are starting from nothing.
- **Lectures closed where it matters.** Exercise 2's design work should be done from the framework in your head, not by copying the worked brief — read the worked brief *after* attempting your own scoping, the same way you consult SOLUTIONS after attempting an exercise.

---

## What these drills add to the portfolio

By the end of the four drills, your `crunchtime-interview-prep-<yourhandle>` repo gains:

- An **audited `frame-writeups/`** — every write-up clears the six-point bar; the README dashboard count is true.
- **`system-design/url-shortener.md`** — the junior-level design write-up, complete through the read/write path.
- **`recruiter-prep/`** — `resume-v3.pdf` (audited), `target-companies.md` (tiered), `outreach-template.md`, `follow-up-template.md`.
- **`study-plan/pre-onsite-4-weeks.md`** — the personalized last-mile plan (the homework's full go-forward plan builds on this).

These four artifacts plus the audited write-ups are four of the seven capstone deliverables. The remaining three — the four mocks, the story bank (from Week 13), and the personalized go-forward plan (the homework) — are the challenges, the prior week, and the homework respectively.
