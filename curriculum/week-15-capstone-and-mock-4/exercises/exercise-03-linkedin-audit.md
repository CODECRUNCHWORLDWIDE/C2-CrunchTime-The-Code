# Exercise 3 — Audit and Rewrite the LinkedIn Levers

**Skill:** LinkedIn for recruiter search (Lecture 3 §2).
**Produces:** the four LinkedIn levers rewritten — headline, About, Featured, skills.
**Target time:** 60 minutes.

## The task

Audit your real LinkedIn profile against the four levers and rewrite each one for recruiter search. By the end you have draft text for the headline, the About section, the Featured caption, and the ordered skills list — ready to paste into LinkedIn.

The framing for the whole exercise: **recruiter search is literal**. A recruiter types a keyword and a location into LinkedIn's search and scans the results. Your profile is a search-optimization problem. Every lever is tuned to the exact phrasings in your target job descriptions.

## Preparation (10 minutes)

Open three or four job descriptions from your target list (Challenge 1). Highlight the recurring phrases — the skills, titles, and technologies that appear in most of them ("Python," "data structures," "distributed systems," "REST APIs," whatever recurs). This highlighted set is your keyword target. Every lever below should contain these exact phrases.

## Specification — the four levers

### Lever 1 — Headline

Rewrite your headline to contain your target keywords and read as a peer, not a hopeful. Cut "aspiring." Pattern: `Role | Key Skills | Building/Domain`.

- **Audit your current headline:** does it contain the keywords a recruiter would search? Does it use "aspiring" or other under-selling language?
- **Rewrite:** keyword-rich, peer-level, under ~120 characters.

### Lever 2 — About

Rewrite the About section in the **first person**, 3–4 short paragraphs: what you do, your thread (the same through-line as your 90-second self-intro), what you are looking for, and the portfolio link. Repeat your target keywords naturally.

- **Audit:** is it empty? Third person? Missing the portfolio link?
- **Rewrite:** first person, 3–4 paragraphs, keywords present, portfolio linked.

### Lever 3 — Featured

Write the one-line caption for the portfolio repo pinned to Featured.

- **Audit:** is anything in Featured? Is the portfolio there?
- **Rewrite:** pin the portfolio; caption it in one line ("60+ algorithm problems solved with the UMPIRE framework — public portfolio").

### Lever 4 — Skills

Order your skills so the **top three** are the ones your target roles filter on.

- **Audit:** are your top three skills the ones recruiters search, or are they random / buried?
- **Rewrite:** list the top three (the ones from your keyword target), then the rest.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Headline** contains at least two target keywords and does not contain "aspiring" or similar under-selling language.
- [ ] **About** is first person, 3–4 paragraphs, contains the target keywords, and links the portfolio.
- [ ] **Featured** has the portfolio pinned with a one-line caption.
- [ ] **Skills** top three match the phrases recruiters filter on for your target roles.
- [ ] Every lever uses the **exact phrasings** from your target job descriptions (not synonyms).

## Self-check

- Search LinkedIn yourself for the role you want, in your metro. Do the profiles that rank look like yours now, or like your rewrite? Match the rewrite to the ones that rank.
- Read your About aloud. Does it sound like a human ("I build...") or a press release ("Jane is a passionate...")? First person, always.
- Cross-check: do the keywords in your headline, About, and skills match each other and match the job descriptions? Inconsistency dilutes the search signal.

## Why this exercise

A recruiter who cannot *find* you cannot interview you, no matter how strong your portfolio. LinkedIn is the channel through which most inbound recruiter interest arrives, and it is purely a search-optimization problem. Forty-five minutes tuning the four levers can be the difference between zero inbound and a recruiter message a week — which, given the funnel math, can be worth more than another ten cold applications.
