# Mini-Projects Index

Every week ends with a small project. They are documented inside each week's `mini-project/` folder. This page is just a quick index.

Unlike a build-things course, most of these produce **write-ups and recordings** rather than applications. That is deliberate: the artifact an interview loop rewards is a solved problem you can narrate, so the mini-projects make you produce the narration and keep it.

## Showcasing your work

Push every mini-project to the portfolio repo you set up in Week 01. By Week 15 you have a public record of fifteen weeks of deliberate practice — write-ups with stated complexity, four recorded mocks, and a story bank.

That repo is the thing to link from your resume. It answers the question a certificate cannot: *can this person reason out loud?*
